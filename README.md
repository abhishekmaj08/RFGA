RFGA: Tumor and Molecular Subtype Mapping in Spatial Transcriptomics
RFGA (Random Forest–Genetic Algorithm) is a supervised machine-learning framework for spot-level tumor identification and molecular subtype mapping in spatial transcriptomics. It combines Random Forest classification with Genetic Algorithm–based feature optimization and incorporates biological reference data and pathology information to generate interpretable spatial predictions.


Highlights: 
Spot-level tumor vs. normal prediction
Spatial mapping of PTC thyroid tumor subtypes: BRAF-like, RAS-like, RET-fusion, and other subtypes
Tumor probability and boundary mapping
Pathology-guided feature optimization
Interpretable gene signatures for downstream biological analysis
